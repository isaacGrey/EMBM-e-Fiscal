# EMBM eFiscal master requirements

EMBM eFiscal is a dedicated Philippine electronic invoicing SaaS developed by EMBM Services OPC. It is commercial and multi-tenant. The official tagline is **Invoice. Integrate. Comply.**

## Permanent product scope

Electronic invoice issuance is the primary purpose. Philippine VAT and applicable tax classifications, verified statutory discount rules, electronic invoice delivery, immutable committed records, authorized audit histories, and BIR e-Invoicing readiness support that purpose. Accounting integration, field mapping, invoice reconciliation, Save & Send Credits, and authorized electronic invoice reporting are supporting capabilities.

EMBM eFiscal complements each subscriber’s accounting software. Priority accounting integrations are MYOB Business, MYOB AccountRight Online, MYOB AccountRight Desktop where supported, QuickBooks Desktop, and QuickBooks Online. Retain AutoCount Accounting and AccountingSuite / 1C in the planned catalog. Priority e-commerce integrations are Shopee Philippines, Lazada Philippines, and TikTok Shop Philippines. Use official, authorized marketplace APIs to retrieve eligible source orders for electronic invoicing. Additional integrations require the project owner’s approval and must support electronic invoicing.

Sales Invoices and Credit Memos remain in scope. Sales Orders are supporting commercial documents. Creating or importing a Sales Order does not issue an electronic invoice or make the order automatically reportable as one.

General accounting, ERP, general transaction management, and all-in-one business management are outside the product scope. Excluded modules include General Ledger, Payroll, Bank Reconciliation, Accounts Payable Management, General Inventory Management, Manufacturing, and Fixed Asset Management.

## Subscriber operations

- Self-service B2B and B2C registration and subscription purchase. Provision accounts only after payment is verified.
- Save & Send Credits, online top-ups, automatic subscription renewal, and optional automatic top-ups for eligible plans.
- Configure monthly and annual billing, Save & Send credit allowances, company file and user limits, integration eligibility, capabilities, and credit packages centrally. Display provisional prices, names, and allowances as illustrative until the owner approves commercial terms.
- Display Save & Send Credits, Available Credits, Used Credits, Included Credits, Additional Credits, Buy Credits, and Credit Usage History in the applicable customer interfaces. Default estimates use monthly Sales Invoices plus monthly Credit Memos, multiplied by 12 for annual totals. Sales Order volume contributes zero. Recommend plans using credit requirements and applicable entitlements.
- The purchasing subscriber becomes the Account Owner only after verified provisioning. Initial registration requests subscriber details and acknowledgment. Company TIN, registered RDO, company files, branches, and taxpayer information belong to company onboarding.
- Customer-managed company files, TIN, registered RDO, branches, users, and permissions. B2B and B2C describe the subscriber relationship and do not restrict invoice recipients.
- Maintain separate identity, commercial, company configuration, regulatory review, and issuance states. Email verification or company configuration cannot activate a paid subscription or authorize regulated issuance.
- Limit team invitations and assignments to authorized companies and grants. Preserve the last Account Owner; use a separate secure ownership transfer. Registered RDO assignments preserve effective-dated history and are independent of customer RDO and regulator grants.
- Sales Invoices, Credit Memos, Sales Orders, customer and item master data, Philippine tax groups, statutory discount groups, and electronic delivery.
- Universal accounting field mapping, automatic accounting synchronization, and reconciliation of committed electronic invoice records against actual connected accounting records.
- Vendor Super Admin management with the same financial immutability rules as each tenant.

## Permanent billing and wallet requirements

Use a versioned PostgreSQL catalog for published monthly and annual PHP prices, credit allocations, company and user limits, connector eligibility, and subscription and package policies. Store prices as exact decimals and credits as whole units. Pin purchased plan and package versions to the checkout and paid period. Publish changed terms as a new version with an effective date. Catalog values remain illustrative until owner approval.

Trust only verified provider confirmation for payment fulfillment. Under a transaction, match the provider, checkout, registered customer, subscriber scope, amount, currency, and status. Provision Account Owner membership, the subscriber subscription, and one shared Save & Send wallet on the first confirmed payment. Allocate subscription and purchased credits at the times authorized by the paid terms. Payment confirmation leaves regulatory verification and issuance authorization independent.

Record credit grants, reservations, releases, consumption, expiry, and authorized corrections in an append-only ledger. Use PostgreSQL locking, idempotency constraints, and compensating entries. Spend the eligible lot that expires first, then the oldest effective eligible lot. Historical access and statutory retention survive subscription expiration. An approved statutory exception requires a documented authority and a scoped audit trail before any issuance bypass is enabled.

Require separate, revocable consent for auto-renewal and automatic top-ups. Store only authorized provider payment method references. Enforce configured package eligibility and spending limits before recurring charges. Apply scheduled plan changes on confirmed renewal within the configured entitlement limits. Proration, real refunds, recurring collection and tax invoicing for EMBM subscription purchases require approved policies and a verified provider. Development payments and local vendor operations are gated to Development.

## Finality and credit use

Draft creation, Save Draft, transaction auto-save, and persistent uncommitted transactions are prohibited for all document types. Committed documents cannot be edited, deleted (including soft deletion), reopened, or overwritten, including by Vendor Super Admin. Corrections require authorized separate linked transactions or lifecycle events. External accounting changes cannot overwrite committed records. Historical committed records remain accessible subject to applicable retention and access requirements.

| Document | Final action | Server action | Default credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales Invoice | Save & Send | CommitAndSend | 1 |
| Credit Memo | Save & Send | CommitAndSend | 1 |
| Sales Order | Save | CommitOnly | 0 |

- For a Sales Invoice or Credit Memo, validate the document, permissions, applicable accounting mappings, and available credits. Commit the new record permanently and consume the applicable credits atomically. Queue automatic accounting synchronization and applicable electronic delivery, and prepare reporting only where authorized. A Credit Memo must reference the applicable original invoice and preserve it unchanged.
- For a Sales Order, validate the order, subscription entitlement, permissions, and applicable mappings. Permanently commit with Save, enqueue automatic accounting synchronization, and record sync status and audit history. Sales Orders have no Save & Send action, credit balance check, reservation, deduction, electronic invoice delivery, or BIR transmission. A zero wallet balance alone cannot block Save. Cancellation, fulfillment, closure, and replacement use separate authorized lifecycle events.
- A new Sales Invoice may be entered directly, reference an authorized external Sales Order, or reference a committed eFiscal Sales Order. Neither source order uses or reserves credits. Charge for the resulting invoice only on successful Save & Send. Enforce authorized fulfillment and invoicing limits and preserve the source order.

Only successfully committed billable Save & Send operations use credits. Enforce the action server-side by document type, within the tenant and authorized permissions. Require idempotency keys to prevent duplicate charges and commit the record and credit deduction together. Track commitment, accounting synchronization, electronic delivery, BIR reporting, and credit consumption independently. For a Sales Order, the last three states are Not Applicable.

Failed validation and other pre-commit attempts, Sales Order creation and saving, external Sales Order retrieval, populating an invoice form from a source order, ordinary saving, PDF and other previews, email template editing, test email, calculations, printing, PDF downloads, accounting synchronization and retries, resending, delivery and BIR reporting retries, reconciliation, field mapping, master data maintenance, and viewing historical documents use zero credits. Operational email and test rate limits are independent of the wallet. A delivery failure after successful commitment retains the original charge. Retry without charging again.

## Independent company workspaces

Every subscriber has an independent electronic invoicing workspace. Each authorized company file has separate Sales Invoice, Credit Memo and Sales Order workstations, customer and item master data, tax and discount groups, numbering settings, sender settings, email templates, Sales Invoice and Credit Memo PDF templates, accounting connections, field mappings, transaction records and authorized users. Multiple authorized employees may work concurrently in a company. Every sensitive operation must validate tenant and company scope on the server. The subscriber wallet is shared across eligible company files.

## Company master data and financial calculations

- Authorize master data and its references against the subscriber and company. Preserve individual customer records without a TIN or RDO where these fields are inapplicable. Keep an immutable revision for each edit and use inactive status for retirement. Future document commitment must copy the terms, identifiers, prices, exchange rates, and other snapshots it uses.
- Resolve customer overrides before customer group defaults. For line tax and discount defaults use an explicit selection, then the item, customer, and customer group. Resolve prices in the requested currency using the effective terms. Prefer a matching customer tier, then a customer-group tier, then the default list's general tier, then the item's base price and configured unit conversion. An explicitly selected list limits the search. Within the same specificity prefer the selected or default list, the highest eligible minimum quantity, and the latest effective date.
- Select the most recent effective revision of a tax group, discount group, price list, or price entry for the transaction date. A later expiry or deactivation closes that revision. Company authorization and verified scenario conditions govern statutory calculations. Effective company dates and the supported source coverage are separate.
- Calculate with exact decimal values. Preserve separate VATable, exempt, zero-rated, and non-VAT amounts, output VAT, withholding, gross total, and net due. Apply commercial line discounts in configured order before document discount allocation. Allocate document discounts proportionally in currency minor units and break remainder ties by line order. Statutory rules supply their own calculation sequence and evidence conditions.
- Run price and calculation previews in a read-only database transaction. The document workstations use the same service during authorized commitment. Sales Invoice and Sales Order can explicitly choose an active company payment term before applying customer and group defaults. Credit Memo calculations use the linked invoice’s committed terms and present positive magnitudes with a reversal direction. Formal issuance, PDF, delivery and regulator workflows remain governed by their respective requirements.

## Sender and document template configuration

- Each company controls sender name, verified sender identity, reply-to, provider, and authorized CC and BCC. Support platform-managed delivery, custom SMTP with secure TLS and the provider's supported authentication mode, and supported OAuth providers through adapters. Require verified sender identity or appropriate provider authorization before sending. Provide connection validation, test email with fictional data, and delivery attempt history.
- Credentials and OAuth tokens are encrypted on the backend with managed keys and never returned through APIs. Custom connections must reject internal, loopback, link-local and other prohibited network targets, validate resolved addresses and enforce egress restrictions at connection time. Provider identity must prevent impersonation. Rate-limit tests independently of the wallet.
- Company email templates are separate for Sales Invoices and Credit Memos. Support subject, body, branding, signature, footer and authorized recipients and copies. Allow only known variables, including CompanyName, CustomerName, CustomerEmail, DocumentType, DocumentNumber, DocumentDate, DocumentTotal, AmountDue, DueDate, OriginalInvoiceNumber, SalesOrderReference and CompanyContactInformation. HTML formats must be sanitized before use and never run scripts or arbitrary expressions. Support defaults, version history, and fictional sample previews and tests. Resolve the selected version against the committed document snapshot at delivery time.
- Company PDF settings use a safe declarative schema. Support a logo, registered business and customer layouts, document header and title, line item table and column arrangement, required totals, tax and discount information, font styles, colors and page margins, payment instructions, terms, applicable signature fields, footer and page numbering. Support multiple saved layouts, per-type defaults, duplication, validation, previews, version history and restoration as a new version. Layout choices cannot conceal legally required information or change authoritative financial values. Verify actual Philippine regulatory requirements before production issuance. Review library compatibility and licensing before selecting a server-side PDF renderer.
- Email and template configurations are administrative settings. They may be edited and versioned with audit history. Changes cannot affect committed financial snapshots or prior issued files.

## Committed document delivery and source orders

Save & Send atomically commits the invoice or credit memo, consumes one credit by default, and pins the selected document and email template versions in durable work. After commit, generate the PDF server-side from the immutable snapshot, preserve the issued bytes and integrity hash, render the applicable email, and attach the PDF using the authorized company sender and recipient. Record delivery state and attempt history. Synchronize to accounting automatically and prepare only applicable authorized reporting. PDF generation, email and accounting calls run outside the financial database transaction. A generation or delivery failure preserves the committed record and its original charge. Retries and manual resends reuse the original issued artifact and do not silently change its financial content or cost another credit. PDF delivery remains separate from prescribed structured electronic invoice formats and BIR reporting payloads.

- For an external accounting Sales Order, find the reference through the authorized company connector by number, customer, date range, external identity or supported status. Retrieve the number at minimum. Customer data, item lines, quantities, prices, discounts, tax and remaining invoicable quantity are optional and must reflect verified connector capabilities. Importing a reference can populate only an in-memory Sales Invoice form. It cannot commit the invoice, persist a draft or recreate the external order. Store authorized source evidence and identifiers with committed references.
- An eFiscal Sales Order uses Save, consumes zero credits and synchronizes to the authorized accounting system. Preserve the resulting external ID and reference number. It can supply a later Sales Invoice and never starts electronic invoice delivery or BIR transmission.
- Provide a Sales Order reference selector on the Sales Invoice workstation. Preserve source system, connector and external account identities, external order ID and number, internal order ID if applicable, retrieval timestamp, related invoice identity and applicable line references. Support authorized partial invoicing where the connector supports it. Track invoiced and remaining quantities, reconcile existing external invoices when necessary, and reject unauthorized, duplicate or excessive invoicing. Preserve the order relationship on invoice synchronization where verified connector capabilities permit it.

## Universal API Connector Builder

Vendor Super Admin manages reusable API connector definitions for Accounting Software, E-Commerce, ERP, POS and Custom Business Software. ERP is an external connector category. The module supports manual configuration and reviewed imports of OpenAPI 3.x JSON or YAML, supported Swagger, supported Postman Collection JSON and validated eFiscal connector configurations. Import base URLs, operations, parameters, authentication definitions, typed request and response schemas and required fields for review. Enforce size, format, schema and reference limits. Disable external schema resolution, script execution, automatic request execution and automatic publication.

Each definition has a stable ID and code, display name, approved logo and description, category, supported editions and regions, documentation reference, integration type, version, availability, required authorizations and declared capabilities. Integration types include configurable REST, dedicated cloud adapter, desktop agent, QuickBooks Web Connector and other approved methods. Review capabilities by edition, region and provider authorization. Enforce approved capabilities at runtime. Accounting operations can include company discovery, eligible master-data reads, Sales Order retrieval, Sales Invoice/Credit Memo/Sales Order creation, invoice and memo reads, document status and actual accounting records. Commerce operations can include authorized shop discovery, order and product reads, authorized customer fields, discounts, shipping, refunds, cancellations, settlements and supported invoice-reference updates.

Approved authentication mechanisms include API keys, bearer tokens, Basic over TLS, OAuth 2.0 authorization code and client credentials, token refresh, reviewed provider signing and dedicated bridge authorization. Separate vendor application secrets from scoped client tokens. Store secure references. Implement unsupported authentication with reviewed and deployed adapters. Define each endpoint’s operation, allowed base URL, relative path, HTTP method, headers, query and path parameters, body and response schemas, required permissions, timeout, rate limit, retry, pagination, error mapping and idempotency. Validate effective destinations, redirects and DNS at runtime. Keep local accounting behind authorized eFiscal Bridge on Windows. Use supported QuickBooks Desktop SDK or Web Connector mechanisms.

Integrate request and response mapping with Universal Field Mapping. Support header, detail and custom fields, required field checks, type conversion, defaults, lookups, approved conditional transformations, and tax, discount, currency and unit mapping. Offer versioned vendor defaults and company-specific overrides. Keep committed fiscal values and authoritative financial validation outside the transformation engine. Administrators may author declarative configurations. Custom executable integration code requires separate review, testing and deployment.

The authorized testing workspace validates specifications and schemas, tests approved authentication and endpoints against synthetic or authorized sandbox accounts, previews requests and mappings, displays sanitized responses and errors, and reports timings and rate-limit information. Prohibit automatic production writes from testing. Imports, testing, configuration, retrieval, synchronization and retries use zero Save & Send Credits.

Connector lifecycle uses Configuration Draft, Awaiting Validation, Testing, Validated, Approved, Published, Disabled, Deprecated and Retired. Configuration drafts are administrative records. Preserve historic versions, require review and authorization before publication, control upgrades, and support emergency disablement and revocation. Published eligible versions appear in the client marketplace without a frontend code change. Clients authorize their own accounts, discover permitted company files or shops, bind one to their eFiscal company, validate a company mapping, test and activate the connection. Pin mappings and connections to reviewed versions and apply entitlements, company scope, least privilege and audit logging throughout.

External accounting orders retain their provider identities. Retrieve authorized Sales Orders when supported, synchronize eFiscal-originated orders, and convert eligible orders through the approved invoice commitment workflow. Preserve partial quantity and duplicate controls. Marketplace source records preserve the authorized shop, order ID and reference, dates, authorized buyer data, lines, prices, discounts, shipping, available tax information, refunds and cancellation state. Invoice eligible orders only through explicit, authorized commitment with source identity and quantity controls.

Phase 7 covers the registry, administrative Builder and specification import, capabilities, development accounting and commerce adapters, company resource binding, versioned mapping metadata and eligible source retrieval. Publish only a reviewed development adapter in this phase. Use approved provider evidence before extending publication to production. Phase 8 covers Universal Field Mapping, reference mapping, controlled execution, sandbox testing, synchronization and retry. Every outbound job pins its destination, connector and mapping revisions. Confirm an external record through reliable readback before marking synchronization complete. After an ambiguous write, verify the original operation without replaying it. Source refreshes append observed revisions and preserve source identity. Phase 12 completes vendor approval, publishing, monitoring and version management. Phase 13 covers approved production MYOB, QuickBooks Desktop, QuickBooks Online, Shopee Philippines, Lazada Philippines, TikTok Shop Philippines and other approved integrations after edition, region, access, authorization and capability verification. See the [architecture’s provider references](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#connector-architecture-requirements).

## Regulator access

Only formally authorized reporting and read-only BIR access are in scope. Nationwide BIR access requires formal authorization. Authorized users can filter by registered RDO within their grants. Client and Vendor Super Admin login pages have no RDO field. BIR login can request RDO context without granting permissions. Live BIR transmission remains disabled pending formal authorization. Electronic invoice reporting must use officially supported methods and stay within formal authorization. Never invent government API endpoints or claim BIR accreditation, certification, or direct government connectivity without official verification.

## Phase 10 and 10.1 reporting preparation boundary

The approved scope is BIR Regulatory Compliance and an API-ready Reporting Gateway. The project owner reports that BIR's upcoming transmission API is not available. Keep preparation mode and development simulation available without inventing an official payload, authentication method, endpoint, receipt, or deadline. Internal evidence is explicitly versioned as an EMBM preparation envelope and never represented as a government submission or acceptance. Future integration requires official specifications, verified authorization, reviewed mapping versions and a separately approved transport.

Preparation copies committed Sales Invoice and Credit Memo evidence without changing the fiscal record, original file, wallet, delivery or accounting status. Sales Orders are excluded. Preparation evidence and simulation results are immutable, company scoped and audited. Read-only roles cannot prepare or simulate. Simulation is restricted to synthetic subscribers and documents in Development. All reporting operations use zero Save & Send Credits. Production issuance, BIR sign-in and live government transmission remain disabled.
